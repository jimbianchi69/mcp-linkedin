## Pre-Shoot Checklist

**Gear:**
- [ ] Primary camera body + backup body
- [ ] Lenses: [list — e.g., 24–70mm f/2.8, 70–200mm f/2.8]
- [ ] Gimbal (calibrated with primary lens)
- [ ] DJI Mavic Pro (wide shots)
- [ ] DJI Avata FPV (chase/tracking)
- [ ] Drone batteries (keep warm until launch — inside jacket or heated case)
- [ ] Memory cards (2x what you expect to use)
- [ ] Two external drives for same-day backup
- [ ] ND filter set

**Logistics:**
- [ ] Permits confirmed (resort, National Forest, or private land)
- [ ] Athlete briefing scheduled (day before or morning of shoot)
- [ ] Safety spotter assigned for high-speed tracking shots
- [ ] Walkie-talkies or comms plan for split crew
- [ ] Emergency contact and bail-out plan if weather turns
