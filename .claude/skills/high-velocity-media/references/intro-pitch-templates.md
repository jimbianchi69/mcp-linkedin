# Intro Pitch Templates

These are warm-introduction pitches — not cold outreach. Your contact has already opened the door. Your job is to give the hiring manager or producer a compelling reason to walk through it with you.

**Key principle:** Two targeted pieces beat ten general ones. Lead with the spec work built specifically for their lane, not your full portfolio history.

---

## How to Ask Your Contact for the Introduction

Before you reach out to the hiring manager, make sure your contact has primed them. A good ask:

> "Hey [CONTACT] — I've put together two spec pieces, one aimed specifically at what [Celtics / Religion of Sports] does. Would you be comfortable introducing me to [NAME / whoever makes production hires]? I want to make sure they know the context before I send anything over."

This ensures the hiring manager already knows who you are when your email lands — which changes everything about how it reads.
